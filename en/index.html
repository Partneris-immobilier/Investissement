Why diversify

Three reasons to broaden your real estate strategy

Optimize yield

Gross rental yields range from under 3% in Switzerland to over 10% in some emerging markets. Diversifying helps balance stability and performance.

Spread the risk

Diversifying across several currencies, tax regimes and economic cycles reduces exposure to any single market.

Anticipate the legal framework

Every country imposes its own rules on foreign buyers. Knowing them in advance avoids unpleasant surprises and speeds up the transaction.
