International markets

Diversifying your real estate wealth internationally

From Portugal to Indonesia by way of the United States, gross rental yields range from 2% to over 11% depending on the market. Here's how to structure a coherent international strategy.

Overview

The main market families

Yield 3 – 5%

Mature markets

Western Europe, North America, Singapore, Japan. Strong liquidity, legal security, stable appreciation — at the cost of moderate rental yield.

Yield 5 – 7%

Growth markets

Poland, Portugal, United Kingdom, Latin America. Balance between capital gain potential, solid rental demand and relatively open access for foreigners.

Yield 7%+

High-yield markets

South Africa, Kazakhstan, Turkey, Dominican Republic. High performance, in exchange for increased currency, political or liquidity risk.

Methodology

5 criteria for selecting a market

1. Rental yield

Average gross market yield, to be adjusted for costs, vacancy and local taxation to get a realistic net yield.

2. Legal framework

Restrictions on foreign ownership, nationality conditions, required legal structures (company, long-term lease, fideicomiso...).

3. Currency risk

A high yield in a volatile currency can be offset by that currency's depreciation against the Swiss franc.

4. Political stability

Legal security, respect for property rights, history of nationalizations or exchange controls.

5. Taxation

Tax on rental income, capital gains, transfer duties, and the existence of a double-taxation treaty with Switzerland.

6. Exit liquidity

Ease of resale: depth of the local buyer market, average transaction times, presence of international buyers.

Strategies

Three diversification approaches

Cash flow

Target high-yield markets (Eastern Europe, Latin America, Southeast Asia) to generate net rental income from the first year.

Appreciation

Favor mature markets with strong structural demand (major European or North American metropolises) betting on long-term capital gains.

Residence & taxation

Combine real estate investment with a residence program (golden visa) for wealth optimization and international mobility.
